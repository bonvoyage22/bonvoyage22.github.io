Part 2: The Ultimate 3-Day ARMY Singapore Itinerary

Make the most of your Singapore trip with these three days of futuristic attractions, thrilling experiences, shopping, food, and iconic city views.

DAY 1 · EXPLORE SINGAPORE

🌆 Futuristic Singapore & Changi Takeover

Morning/Afternoon: Jewel Changi Airport

Land at Changi Airport and head straight to Jewel Changi. Marvel at the massive indoor waterfall, The Rain Vortex, and explore the Canopy Park.

It is the perfect first stop for photos, shopping, food, and a memorable start to your Singapore adventure.

Evening: Gardens by the Bay

Head to Gardens by the Bay. Walk through the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome, then catch the nightly Supertree Grove light show, which features stunning music and visual effects.

🌃 City Icons & Skyline Views

💜 Essential Travel Tips for International ARMYs

  • Activate your eSIM early: Check device compatibility and activate your mobile data plan according to the provider's instructions.
  • Plan your MRT journeys: Download a Singapore transport map and check the nearest MRT stations to your hotel and concert venue.
  • Carry a portable power bank: You will probably take hundreds of photos and videos, so keep your phone charged.
  • Stay hydrated: Singapore is hot and humid. Carry a reusable water bottle and wear comfortable clothing.
  • Book attraction tickets early: Reserve popular attractions in advance and check your selected admission dates and times.
  • Keep concert essentials ready: Pack your ARMY Bomb, concert ticket, ID, portable charger, and other permitted items. Check the official venue rules before arriving.
