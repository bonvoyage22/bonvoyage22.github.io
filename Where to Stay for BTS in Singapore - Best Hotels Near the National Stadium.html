Where to Stay for BTS in Singapore: Best Hotels Near the National Stadium

Discover the best hotels near Singapore National Stadium, from affordable stays in Kallang and Geylang to luxury accommodation in Bugis and the City Centre.

Tens of thousands of regional and international ARMYs are flying into Singapore this December. And finding accommodation with easy transit access to the concert at the National Stadium (Kallang), is crucial.

Singapore's humidity and post-concert crowds mean you want to plan your stay strategically to avoid long queues at the MRT station after the show.

The best neighborhoods to stay in are Kallang (right next to the venue), Geylang/Lavender (budget-friendly and walking distance), and Bugis/Marina Bay (a few direct MRT stops away).

💜 Quick Booking Tips for International Fans

  1. Book near the Circle Line (Yellow): The National Stadium is served by Stadium MRT Station (CC6). Booking a hotel near a Circle Line station, such as Promenade, Esplanade, or Dhoby Ghaut, can make traveling to the concert easier. Check the route and transfers before booking.
  2. Use Mobile Apps: Both Agoda and Booking.com sometimes offer mobile-only promotions. Compare the final prices on their websites and apps before completing your reservation.
  3. Book Early: Concert dates can make nearby hotels sell out quickly. Reserve a refundable room early if possible.
  4. Plan Your Return Journey: Expect large crowds around Stadium MRT after the show. Consider walking to your hotel or using alternative transport if your accommodation is nearby.

Final Thoughts: Where Should ARMYs Stay?

If convenience is your priority, choose accommodation around Kallang or Lavender. If you want to save money, Geylang is worth considering. For a more luxurious Singapore holiday with friends, Bugis and the City Centre offer excellent accommodation choices. Wherever you decide to stay, remember to book early, check transport routes, and leave enough time to enjoy the concert experience.
